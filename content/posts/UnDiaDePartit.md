## Opening Night of the New League Phase

Barcelona kick off their **2026/27 UEFA Champions League** campaign tonight against Dutch side **Feyenoord**, in what marks Matchday 1 of the competition's league phase. Hansi Flick's side return to Camp Nou riding a wave of red-hot domestic form, while Feyenoord travel to Catalonia unbeaten in over a month.

Here's the full statistical breakdown — and our final score prediction.

---

## 📊 Form Guide

### 🔵🔴 Barcelona
- **5 wins in a row** across all competitions
- **19 goals scored, only 3 conceded** in that run
- Recent results include a **5-0 demolition of Valencia** and a **5-2 win over Rayo Vallecano**
- Back-to-back clean sheets against Athletic Bilbao and Elche earlier in the run
- Under Hansi Flick, Barça average **2.88 goals per Champions League match** (75 goals in 26 games)
- Flick holds a **72.7% win rate** — the best of any manager with 10+ Champions League appearances

### 🟠⚫ Feyenoord
- **Unbeaten in 14 matches** across all competitions
- **Unbeaten in their last 8 away games**
- **3 wins from 3 on the road this season**, scoring 9 and conceding 3
- Missing several key defenders — **Beelen, Nieuwkoop, Smal, Moder, and Bos** are all out, leaving Van Bronckhorst's backline short-handed

---

## ⭐ Player to Watch: Raphinha

Since the start of the 2024/25 season, **Raphinha has been directly involved in 26 Champions League goals** (16 goals + 10 assists) — second only to Harry Kane in that timeframe. With Feyenoord already thin at the back, he's the man most likely to punish any gaps.

Bookmakers have installed Barcelona as **overwhelming favorites**, with a home win priced as low as **1.12** — an unusually lopsided line even by Champions League standards. Implied probability for a Barça win sits close to **88–89%** before margin adjustment.
